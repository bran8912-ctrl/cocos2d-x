📋 Game Overview

Welcome to your luxury entertainment empire! Build your business from the ground up with strategic decisions, AI-powered events, and dynamic character interactions.

✨ Key Features

🤖 AI-Driven Events

Intelligent system generates unique scenarios that adapt to your play style and learning patterns

👥 Dynamic Characters

Procedurally generated staff and guests with individual traits, preferences, and satisfaction levels

💰 Business Management

Balance finances, staffing, guest satisfaction, and reputation to build a thriving venue

📊 Learning System

The AI learns from your decisions to generate increasingly relevant and personalized scenarios

🎯 Meaningful Choices

Every decision impacts money, reputation, relationships, and long-term business success

📈 Progressive Growth

Expand your operations, hire quality staff, and attract VIP guests as your reputation grows

🎮 How to Play

  • Manage Your Business: Monitor your finances, staff satisfaction, and guest experience
  • Make Strategic Decisions: Each day presents unique challenges requiring thoughtful choices
  • Build Relationships: Keep your team happy and your guests satisfied for recurring revenue
  • Grow Your Reputation: Higher reputation attracts better guests and opportunities
  • Adapt to AI Scenarios: Respond to procedurally generated events specific to your playstyle
  • Achieve Success: Balance short-term profits with long-term business growth

🌟 Game Mechanics

  • Money: Earn from guests, spend on staff and expansion, invest in improvements
  • Reputation: Grows with good decisions, attracts premium guests and partnerships
  • Staff Satisfaction: Affects retention, service quality, and guest experience
  • Guest Loyalty: Happy guests return more often and spend more money
  • Daily Operations: Each day brings income/expenses and new decision opportunities

💡 Tips for Success

  • Invest early in staff training to improve service quality
  • Balance aggressive expansion with financial stability
  • Pay attention to character preferences and event patterns
  • Build reputation gradually - it attracts high-value guests
  • Monitor cash reserves carefully for unexpected opportunities
  • The AI learns your style - adapt your strategy over time
